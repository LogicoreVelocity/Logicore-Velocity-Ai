# Branding

Use the clear public name:

```text
Logicore Velocity AI
```

Use LV only as a small icon/monogram, not as the legal/public product name. Two-letter marks are usually weak and can conflict easily.

Recommended tagline:

```text
Know your operations. Manage your fleet. Anywhere, anytime.
```

Avoid unverified claims such as:

```text
First vendor intelligence engine
Legally certified
Fully compliant
Guaranteed secure
```

Acceptable wording:

```text
Open-source local import/export workspace for operations teams.
Privacy-aware, tenant-ready templates for drivers, fleet, finance, and payroll workflows.
```
